## Source Quality Rules

- Prefer FIFA and government pages for dates, rules, tickets, and entry requirements.
- Use airlines, airport, venue, and city transit pages for operational details.
- Treat blogs, social posts, and resale sites as secondary context only.
- If a fact affects money, entry, or match attendance, cite the source and date checked.
