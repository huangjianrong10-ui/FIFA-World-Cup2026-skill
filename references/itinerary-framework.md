# Itinerary Framework

## Traveler Inputs

Collect these fields before proposing a route:

- Departure city in Taiwan and preferred airline constraints.
- Travel window and maximum days off.
- Match priority: specific team, host city, knockout stage, or atmosphere.
- Budget band in TWD.
- Group size and room-sharing preference.
- U.S. travel experience and driving comfort.
- Accessibility, dietary, medication, or language needs.

## Route Patterns

### West Coast Entry

Best for Los Angeles, San Francisco Bay Area, Seattle.

- Good for shorter Taiwan-to-U.S. travel time.
- Easier to combine two cities without crossing the continent.
- Useful when the fan values lower jet lag and simpler transfers.

### Central Cluster

Best for Dallas, Houston, Kansas City, Atlanta.

- Useful for match-heavy plans.
- Domestic flights may be more practical than trains.
- Weather and heat planning matter in June and July.

### East Coast Cluster

Best for New York New Jersey, Philadelphia, Boston, Miami.

- Good for fans who want city sightseeing plus matches.
- New York New Jersey, Philadelphia, and Boston can be grouped more naturally than Miami.
- Lodging prices may move sharply around major match days.

## Budget Model

Show assumptions and ranges rather than a single number.

| Item | Suggested Estimate Method |
| --- | --- |
| International airfare | Taiwan-U.S. round trip, seasonality-adjusted |
| Domestic transport | Flights/trains/buses between host cities |
| Lodging | Nights x room rate x room count |
| Match tickets | Ticket count x assumed tier |
| Food | Days x per-person daily estimate |
| Local transit | Days x city transit/rideshare estimate |
| Insurance and eSIM | Per traveler |
| Reserve | 10% to 15% of subtotal |

## Final Review Checklist

- Dates match official FIFA schedule.
- City and venue names are current.
- ESTA/passport guidance is written as a reminder, not a guarantee.
- Taiwan Mandarin is natural and service-oriented.
- Costs include assumptions, currency, and refresh date.
- The plan includes what to book first and what can wait.
